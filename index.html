Precision Airgun Suite (PAS) Privacy Policy

Developer / publisher: AirBallistics UK
Effective date: 4 September 2026
Privacy contact: airballisticsuk@gmail.com

1. Overview

Precision Airgun Suite is designed to operate locally on the user's Android device. PAS does not require a PAS account or an internet connection for normal operation.

PAS contains no advertising, developer analytics or user-tracking functionality and does not itself upload rifle, projectile, ballistic, field-reference or note data to AirBallistics UK or to an AirBallistics UK server.

2. Data PAS accesses and uses locally

PAS may access and store information that the user enters or generates in the app, including ballistic settings, projectile selections, measured point-of-impact data, calculated results, display and chart preferences, optional rifle / scope / projectile reference details, and optional notes.

This information is used locally to provide PAS calculations, charts, comparisons, saved preferences and field-reference outputs. PAS does not require a name, email address, telephone number, location or other account identity to use the app.

Normal PAS working data is stored within PAS application storage on the Android device. AirBallistics UK does not receive or have access to this locally stored data through the app.

3. Network access and Android permissions

PAS is designed without the Android INTERNET permission and without broad storage, camera, microphone or location permissions. Normal ballistic calculations, trajectory charts, ballistic-coefficient fitting, comparisons and field-reference generation are performed locally on the device.

4. Android backup and device transfer

On first use, PAS lets the user choose whether eligible PAS application data may participate in Android backup / device transfer or should remain local to the device.

If the user chooses Allow Android backup / device transfer, eligible PAS application data may be processed by Android / Google backup or device-transfer services according to the user's Android device and Google Account settings and Google's applicable privacy terms. PAS does not operate or control Google's backup service, and AirBallistics UK does not receive access to the user's Google backup.

If the user chooses Keep PAS data local only, PAS does not supply its application data to future Android backup / device-transfer operations. Changing the PAS setting does not itself delete a backup that may already have been created by Android / Google; existing backups are managed through Android / Google backup settings.

5. Save, Share and Print

PAS can create field-reference and comparison documents when the user requests them. Saving uses Android's file-location interface, sharing uses the Android share interface, and printing uses the Android print system.

These actions are initiated by the user. PAS does not automatically send these documents to AirBallistics UK. An app, service, printer or destination selected by the user may apply its own privacy and data-handling terms.

6. Advertising, analytics and tracking

PAS contains no advertising. PAS does not include AirBallistics UK analytics or user-tracking functionality and does not create a PAS user account.

7. Data sharing and sale

AirBallistics UK does not sell PAS user data. PAS does not automatically share locally stored rifle, projectile, ballistic, field-reference or note data with AirBallistics UK or third parties. User-initiated Save, Share and Print actions and the optional Android backup / device-transfer choice are described above.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored PAS data remains on the device until it is changed or removed by the user, Android clears the app's storage, or PAS is uninstalled. Clearing PAS app storage or uninstalling PAS removes PAS local data from that device.

Backups already held by Android / Google are controlled through the user's Android / Google backup settings and are subject to Google's data-retention practices. PAS cannot directly delete an existing Google-held backup.

Because PAS has no PAS account and no AirBallistics UK-hosted user database, AirBallistics UK does not hold an online PAS account record for the user to request deletion of.

9. Security

PAS is designed to minimise data exposure by performing normal calculations locally and blocking normal in-app network loading. Users should still protect their Android device and any files that they choose to save, share, print or include in Android backup.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if PAS functionality, data handling or Google Play requirements change. The current policy will be published at the Privacy Policy address shown in the Google Play listing and will remain accessible from within PAS.
